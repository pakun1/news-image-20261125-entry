A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Wednesday 25 November
2026. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Sum up 25 November's world news
in one image" bounty, $100 in USDC.

## What the image shows

The front page leads with the day's biggest story — the COP31 climate talks
in Antalya, Türkiye, collapsing without an agreement on fossil fuels — and
covers five more stories from the day:

- **Climate** — COP31 talks collapse with no deal on fossil fuels; UN
  climate chief Simon Stiell voiced deep disappointment.
- **Middle East** — a Houthi drone strike hit Riyadh's tallest tower,
  wounding 11, the second attack on the PIF Tower in days.
- **War** — Ukraine confirmed it was behind a drone strike on a Russian
  cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland.
- **Tech** — a US jury convicted Huawei of conspiring to steal trade
  secrets and bank fraud.
- **Business** — Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive.
- **Security** — a hack struck unclassified systems at the CIA and the
  director of national intelligence.

## How it was built

The front page was drawn with Python (Pillow): masthead, date line, six
stories with headlines and short summaries, and one generated illustration
of the empty COP31 summit hall. The text is drawn with real fonts, so every
word is legible at a glance, and every fact in it was reported on
25 November 2026. `main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` to the
grader's output directory.
